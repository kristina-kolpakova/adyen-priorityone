## Known limitations

- **Many accounts tie on their total score (temporary; scoring logic still to be fixed).** An account's priority is currently the higher of its Renewal and Upsell/New scores. Because those scores come only from value, quarter and stage, many accounts reach the same maximum: today six accounts tie at 90 (Kestrel, Krakatoa, Lanternfly, Mosaic, Saltmarsh, Willowbank). Tied accounts are listed alphabetically, so which of them make the top 5 is decided by name, not priority. This must be fixed in the scoring logic, for example with Gong signals or a deliberate tie-break, before the ranking is relied on.
- **Tie-break for equal opportunity scores is undecided.** When two opportunities in the same account have the same `PriorityScore`, it's not yet decided whether the larger deal or the sooner-closing deal should rank first. For now, ties are broken by CSV row order (`rank(method="first")`), which is deterministic but has no business reason behind it. This only changes an account's score when its top two opportunities tie and close in different quarters.
- **Every additional opportunity counts, and deal size has limited weight.** Each extra opportunity adds a share of its `PriorityScore` (50% current quarter or overdue, 25% next quarter, 10% later). Because stage and timing make up most of `PriorityScore`, a small deal can add as much as a large one: Marlowe's €45K deal adds 30 points, while Kestrel's €1.23M future deal adds 6. No cap on additional opportunities is applied yet; this will be revisited after Gong signals are added.
- **Scores depend on the date the script runs.** Scoring uses today's date (`pd.Timestamp.today()`), by design, so priorities stay current. The mock data, however, is a snapshot from mid-November 2026: as time passes, more deals show as overdue, and from 1 January the current quarter becomes Q1 2027, so quarter scores and the ranking shift. Score checks that expect fixed totals (e.g. Krakatoa = 90) will fail after the quarter changes. Automated scoring tests will need a controllable reference date.
